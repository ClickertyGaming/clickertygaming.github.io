This short arrangement is what Clutterswing was born out of. I shared this with my friends on Discord, and I liked it so much, I wanted to make a full remix out of it as soon as possible.

This was basically a test, if anything. I wanted to see how good I was at composing for solo piano, so I picked out Clutterfunk and made a short rearrangement of it. At the bottom of the Clutterswing page, I mention a Geometry Dash level I've been working on for a long time. Yeah, that's the reason. Nothing too special to say about this one, really.

I did try my best to make it, like, *actually* playable, y'know? It's certainly difficult, but I made sure it was viable for a human to actually be able to play this out in full. That's why I added the sheet music for this alongside the standard FLAC and MP3 downloads. Speaking of the sheet music (and subsequently, the actual exported recording), all of that was made in MuseScore 4, and the piano is the Muse Sounds Grand Piano.
