This song is what I would consider a fairly big milestone for me. Electro swing has long been my favorite music genre ever, and I wanted to become better at producing my own songs within it; The greatest compliment an artist can give themselves is saying they consume their own stuff like a regular consumer.

I wanted to get to that point, so I started making music in the genre. It wasn't that good for a long while, but that's just what an art journey is like. You gotta make bad shit before you make the good shit.

The reason I call this song a big milestone is because it was the first instance where I actually started listening to it regularly like any other song I have downloaded on my phone. I've gotten close with other songs I made in the past, because even though I don't listen to them much, I remember feeling proud of them when I first made them. That is not the case with Clutterswing—I genuinely do keep playing it on my phone and listening to it during road trips because it's just that good to me. And I made the damn thing! How cool is that!? This has never happened to me before!

As for the song itself, it's... well, yes, it's a remix of (and a love letter to) **[Clutterfunk](https://www.newgrounds.com/audio/listen/505816)** by Waterflame (who is my favorite Newgrounds musician), that much is obvious. But quite a bit of it, while derivative, is my own rearrangements of different parts of the song. Hell, I got the idea for the electro swing remix because I made a short jazz stride arrangement for solo piano that this is based off of (which you can find **[HERE](/music/1.5/clutterswing-playable)**). Also, the song name is stupid because I had to think of it on the spot. Bite me.

I don't expect anyone to like this as much as I do, people have their own tastes. Hell, you don't even have to like me. I won't be too happy about it, but it's not like I can control you. Do you even like me? No? What the hell are you doing on this website, then? Reading what I have to say about the song that I made? Who would willingly subject themselves to that? That's stupid.

Anyway, thanks for reading. If you wanna financially support me for whatever reason, there's currently no way to do that. Oh well. See you next time when I talk about the Geometry Dash level I've been working on on and off for more than 2 years that I totally managed to build enough of to the point of publicly sharing any sneak peeks.
