It took a bit, but the music archive has now officially been implemented into the site! This will serve as the primary place where I "publish" all of my music (if you can even call it publishing) instead of me having to go through the impossible hurdle that is formal music publishers and streaming services. It means I won't be able to make money off it at the moment, but that's fine. Streaming services barely pay anything anyways, if at all.

Either I also start publishing on Bandcamp or I implement a way to directly send me money through here. The first one seems easier, it's just gonna be annoying with all of the restrictions with free downloads and limited uploads and whatnot that comes with being a free user. Gotta pay the bills, I get that, but still. If I go through with that, I'll update all of the music pages to include Bandcamp links as well as direct downloads and a Newgrounds link here and there.

Oh yeah, music pages. Each entry has a webpage tied to it, containing the aforementioned links and my thoughts on the music itself, the process, some other stuff tied to it, etc. It's a peek into my psyche, the enigmas that are the machinations devised within my mind. I doubt you'll read much of it, but eh. Better to have those thoughts out there.

I should also upload music videos to my YouTube channel, too. That could be a cool thing that can be done once I get enough time and motivation. We both know that's not happening anytime soon, though. Whatever, a guy can dream. As if that's something that I can't do normally in my free time.
