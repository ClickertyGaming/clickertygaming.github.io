as i'm writing this the US government had hosted debates on whether or not they should commit the, frankly, absolutely stupid decision of repealing section 230 of US law. if you don't know, section 230 is a law that provides immunity to online platforms from taking liability for content posted by its users. because of it, they can moderate that content without being responsible as publishers of that content. it is the main thing that keeps the internet functional as it is today. without it, the web would quite literally cease to exist

why am i saying this? because things like independently hosted blogs would survive since they already represent the opinions of the person who hosts and publishes them. if this somehow goes into effect, we'll very likely see a rise of blog sites again. as cool and awesome and tubular as that would be, i don't want the decision to pass. i kind of rely on the internet to keep in contact with everyone i know and love, so, y'know.
